About this app

RGCIPZAH is a private, personal automation tool built and operated by its owner. It schedules and publishes short-form video edits to the owner's own social channels (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram) under the RGCIPZAH brand.

It is not a public web service: it has no user accounts, collects no data from visitors, and interacts only with the owner's own accounts via official platform APIs.

Contact

Use the support email configured in the app's OAuth consent screen, or reach the owner on the GitHub organization page.
