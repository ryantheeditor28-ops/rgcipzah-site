Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. What this app is

RGCIPZAH is a private automation tool operated by a single owner. It uses official platform APIs (YouTube Data API, Facebook/Meta Graph API, Instagram Graph API) to publish short-form video edits to the owner's own channels under the RGCIPZAH brand. It is not a public service and has no end users.

2. Data the app accesses

  • Google/YouTube: OAuth consent is used only for the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly, to upload videos to and read basic info from the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram): Page-scoped tokens are used to publish Reels to the owner's own Facebook Page and linked Instagram account.

3. How data is stored and shared

  • OAuth tokens are stored privately by the owner (in private repositories' encrypted secrets and a local, private credential file). They are never sold, rented, or shared.
  • The app does not collect, process, or store any data about people who visit this website or view the published videos.
  • No data is used for advertising or profiling.

4. Revocation

Access can be revoked at any time: Google account permissions at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Facebook/Instagram via Business Integrations in account settings, or by contacting us.

5. Contact

Use the support email configured in the app's OAuth consent screen, or reach the owner via the GitHub organization page.
